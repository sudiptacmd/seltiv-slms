# Seltiv Walkthrough Design

## Style Prompt
Quiet-premium editorial product film: warm paper, ink-black type, restrained teal and magenta accents, real product screens, deliberate page-turn motion, and generous negative space.

## Colors
- `#faf9f5` — paper background
- `#201e1d` — primary ink
- `#0088b0` — action teal
- `#d6006c` — CTA magenta
- `#e2e0da` — rules and borders

## Typography
- Georgia — authoritative editorial display voice
- Arial — quiet, highly legible supporting voice

## What NOT to Do
- No generic dark-tech gradients or neon.
- No frantic cuts or unreadable UI zooms.
- No abstract filler visuals.
- No unsupported product claims.
