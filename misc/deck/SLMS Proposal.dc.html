Where things stand today

01010101

Questions that are hard to answer today

One student's progress

Results, attendance and fees live in separate registers, so no single view shows how one student is doing across three terms. A child who starts slipping is noticed late, when the term report finally arrives.

The real attendance picture

Attendance is recorded on paper and summarised much later. Which class is drifting, and which student is repeatedly absent, is not visible until the month has closed and the pattern is already set.

Where fee collection stands

Each school keeps its own ledger of who has paid and who is behind. Building one collection figure for the trust means asking four offices and reconciling the answers by hand.

4444

Four schools on one system. Every student, teacher, attendance record and fee sits in the same database — each school keeps its own reports, and the trust gets a single consolidated dashboard across all four.

How the system is built

02020202

Four portals, one database

Admin Portal

Admissions and enrolment, notices, exam schedules, gradesheet publishing, roles and permissions, and the analytics dashboard.

Teacher Portal

Class roll call, gradesheet upload and the timetable. Teacher attendance itself is taken on fingerprint or ID card.

Parent Portal

The child's profile, results, attendance, notices and certificates, plus tuition payment through bKash.

Accounts Portal

Fee collection and dues, instalments and reminders, staff salary and payslips, bKash reconciliation.

The screens

03030303

Admin — Analytics Dashboard

Attendance, fee collection and pass rate across all four schools on one page, with any single school viewable on its own.

Admin — Student Profile

One student’s results, attendance and fee status on a single page — the view that is missing today.

Teacher — Attendance

The teacher marks roll call in class; the record reaches the dashboard and the parents the same minute.

Parent — Child Profile

Parents can see their own child’s results, attendance and notices at any time, without calling the office.

Parent — Fee Payment (bKash)

Fees are paid through bKash, the receipt is issued immediately, and the accounts office sees the transaction already reconciled.

Accounts — Payroll & Payslip

Staff salary processing and payslip generation in one place — payslips belong to staff, not to students.

Admin — Notices & SMS

A notice is written once: it publishes to the portal and goes out by SMS to the selected parents.

Admin — Admission & Enrollment

From the online application form through entrance test, document verification and seat confirmation — one tracked process.

Parent — Service Requests

Transfer certificates and testimonials are requested online and tracked all the way to approval.

Integrations and access control

IntegrationWhat it covers
Payment gatewaybKash for tuition collection, reconciled into Accounts
SMS gatewayNotices, fee reminders and absence alerts
Biometric / RFIDMandatory for teachers, optional for students
Access controlRole-based permissions with an audit log

Every grade edit, fee change and user-permission change is stored with who made it and when, so a sensitive correction can always be traced back.

Optional and future modules

Available now, optional in Phase 1

  • Online Exam & Question Bank — MCQ tests, auto-grading, a reusable question bank, seat plans and invigilation duty
  • Transport Management — bus routes, live GPS for parents, driver and attendant records

Phase 2, for later consideration

  • Library management
  • HR module — leave, increments, provident fund, staff appraisal
  • LMS — assignments, recorded lectures, online class links
  • WhatsApp notifications, visitor and gate management, alumni portal
  • Bangla / English language toggle and offline-first mode
